How RosCAM works

Drop DXFs of flat parts (each part's top face), pick a sheet, and RosCAM arranges them on it clear of its used areas, then writes one program per bit: drills first, then the 1/4" end mill for bigger holes, pockets and the outlines with tabs. The CAM runs in your browser; DXFs are never uploaded.

Simulation

The path the router takes, one color per bit (warm colors drill, cool colors mill). Bright is already cut, faded is still to come, grey lines are rapid moves. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, middle-drag to pan.
