RosCAM

Drop a DXF of a flat plate's top face. RosCAM writes one program per bit: drills first, then the 1/4" end mill for bigger holes, pockets and the outline with tabs. Everything runs in your browser; the DXF is never uploaded anywhere.

Loading the CAM engine. The first visit takes about 15 seconds.
